1. Finish the lesson open in your browser: *The Resilient Model Call* (`/r/ai-programming/learn/aip-resilient-call`).

### Step 1 — Day 1: diagnostics (30 minutes)
1. Switcher → **Prompt Eng** → Assessment → take the 12-question diagnostic honestly (guessing inflates nothing — conservative credit only).

4. Look at **Today**: it should now reflect both roadmaps' next actions. Journal: does the plan match your intuition? (This exact note is P5 input.)

### Step 2 — Days 2–7: the daily loop (45 min/day)
Every session, in this order:
1. **Today** → check streak, due cards, the day's plan blocks.
2. **Review first** — clear due cards before learning new ones (both Beta decks).
3. **Learn** — one module or the next lesson:
   - PE order: *What the Model Actually Sees* → *JSON You Can Trust* → modules in phase order.
   - AIP order: Phase 1 modules → *The Resilient Model Call* (done) → *What a Token Costs* → Phase 2.
4. **Practise** — Day 4 or 5, do ONE practice build: PE's *Role precedence lab* (2h) or AIP's *The fake model provider* (4h). Follow its steps literally; the "done when" is the finish line.
5. **Journal** — two lines minimum: what you learned, one friction note (template below).
6. Mark progress **honestly** — Learning when learning, Practising only when doing its work. The platform's no-fake-progress rule starts with you.

**Friction note template** (journal or a notes file):
> Where: /r/<roadmap>/<page> — What happened: <one sentence> — What I wished: <one sentence>

### Step 3 — Day 7: weekly review (20 minutes)
1. Dashboard → weekly report (hours vs plan, cards, streak).
2. Re-read your 6+ friction notes; star the three that cost you the most time or annoyance.
3. Journal one paragraph: "What I would change first about Today/Planner" — this is the Phase 5 seed.

### Step 4 — Days 8–13: deepen + one flagship each
1. Continue the daily loop (Step 2).
2. Open ONE flagship tracker per roadmap and fill in the **Planning** row:
   - PE: *Production Extraction Service* (its Phase-2 checkpoint is closest).
   - AIP: *Typed LLM Gateway* — you have already built half its milestones as practice builds.
3. Read its milestones + interview questions once (do not start building; just let it steer attention).
4. Do one more practice build this week (choose by interest, not order).
5. Keep the friction notes coming — especially anything about **switching roadmaps** and **review load** (both are P5/P6 design questions).

### Step 5 — Day 14: decide (30 minutes)
1. Dashboard monthly view; count: modules done, lessons read, cards reviewed, practice builds shipped, days streaked. These are your first real numbers on the platform.
2. Rank your starred friction notes.
3. Pick the next build session from PART B (or hand me the ranked list and let me recommend).
4. Journal the decision + why. Phase 4 ends here; Phase 5 begins with your notes, not my guesses.
